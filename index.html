How to use the feed

1️⃣ Observe: read the setup without deciding immediately.
2️⃣ Map liquidity: identify what price may be reaching for.
3️⃣ Check structure: HTF context and local BOS/shift.
4️⃣ Validate the FVG: fresh, displaced and clearly defined.
5️⃣ Plan risk: define invalidation and realistic target.
6️⃣ Decide: Long, Short or No Trade — then use Replay to reveal.
The feed is designed to give you more repetitions. It should train your process, not replace your judgement or create an assumption that a high score means a profitable trade.
